2. Information we may collect

When you contact us through WhatsApp or place an order, we may process:

  • Your name and WhatsApp/telephone number.
  • Your messages and the details you provide about an order.
  • Food selections, quantities, prices, requested date and delivery time.
  • Delivery address and instructions, where needed to fulfil an order.
  • Order, payment-status and customer-service records. Bank-transfer verification is performed manually; please do not send full card details or banking passwords.
  • Technical event and security records needed to operate and troubleshoot the ordering service.

Only information actually collected by the business and its service providers should remain in this list.

3. How we use information

We use relevant information to respond to enquiries, show menus, prepare and manage regular or party orders, check availability, arrange delivery, provide payment instructions, verify payments, handle changes or complaints, maintain business records, protect the service from misuse and meet legal obligations.

We do not use this ordering service for marketing campaigns under the planned initial scope. If that changes, the business must update its practices and this policy as required.
